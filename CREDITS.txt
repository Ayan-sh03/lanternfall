LANTERNFALL - third-party assets
================================

Everything listed here is free to use. The audio is Creative Commons Zero
(CC0, public domain dedication): no attribution is required, but the people who
made it deserve the mention. The fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
their licence texts sit next to them in fonts/.

All pixel art (characters, monsters, tiles, props) is drawn in code by the game
itself (scripts/art.gd) and is original to this project.

SOUND EFFECTS  (CC0)                     audio/sfx/
---------------------------------------------------
Kenney (kenney.nl): "RPG Audio", "Impact Sounds", "Interface Sounds"
    slash, hit, punch, pop, page, step, clank, ui, ui_ok, type, some of coins / bell / thud
rubberduck (OpenGameArt.org): "80 CC0 RPG SFX", "100 CC0 SFX", "100 CC0 SFX #2"
    slash3, kill, growl, roar, slime, shoot, bolt, fire, cinder, relic, lock, stairs,
    chest, urn, boom, gong, dash, thunder, some of coins / bell / thud

The files were trimmed, converted to mono OGG and level-matched for the game.
A handful of cues (level-up, heart, lamp chime, telegraph tones, and others) are
still synthesised at startup by scripts/sfx.gd, which also holds a full
synthesised fallback soundtrack used only if a music file is missing.
