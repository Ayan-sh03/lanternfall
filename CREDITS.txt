LANTERNFALL - third-party assets
================================

Everything listed here is free to use and to redistribute with the game.
Most of the audio is Creative Commons Zero (CC0, public domain dedication): no
attribution is required, but the people who made it deserve the mention. Three
music tracks are under licences that do require credit (CC-BY 3.0, CC-BY 4.0,
OGA-BY 3.0); they are marked below and this file ships inside every build. The
fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; their licence texts sit next to
them in fonts/.

All pixel art (characters, monsters, tiles, props) is drawn in code by the game
itself (scripts/art.gd) and is original to this project.

The files were trimmed, converted to mono OGG and level-matched for the game.
A handful of cues (level-up, heart, lamp chime, telegraph tones, and others) are
still synthesised at startup by scripts/sfx.gd, which also holds a full
synthesised fallback soundtrack used only if a music file is missing.
